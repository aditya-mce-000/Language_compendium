Maggi Masala

This is the recipe of maggi masala.

Ingredients

  • Maggi
  • Salt
  • Turmeric Powder
  • Water

Instructions

  1. Boil the maggi for 5 minutes at 80C.
  2. While boiling, put in the maggi masala at the third minute of boiling.
  3. Put on salt and turmeric according to preffered taste after tasting the soup at fourth minute.
  4. Serve in a bowl with good amount of soup.
